## Claims that cannot currently be supported

| Unsupported claim | Missing evidence / responsible next step |
| --- | --- |
| Clinically useful or accurate next-24-hour prediction; specific sensitivity/AUC benefit | No eligible real research bundle or prospective/external evaluation. An approved research team must establish evidence and a prespecified study. |
| Causal trigger discovery, diagnosis, treatment efficacy or emergency monitoring | No causal/clinical/intervention study or monitored service; retain descriptive and user-initiated language. |
| Generalization across people or populations | Single-account evaluation only; statistician must design grouped/time evaluation, class sufficiency and dependence-aware uncertainty. |
| Real research consent/enrollment, approved data acquisition or author collaboration | No consent/adjudication ledger or approved institutional team. Access inquiry and pilot are drafts requiring separate authorization. |
| All history immutable, complete or reconstructable | Legacy revisions absent; app journal is not administrator-proof. Independent source/retention/withdrawal review is required. |
| Anonymous exports or effective deletion from all copies | Exact timelines remain sensitive; downloaded copies/backups are outside app erasure. A privacy owner must define lifecycle controls. |
| Public deployment, production security, PostgreSQL/TLS/role verification or encrypted restore readiness | Existing records explicitly leave host execution, operational ownership and independent sign-off unresolved. |
| Full accessibility or migraine-time comfort | Selected automated checks only; manual assistive-technology, device and participant usability review remains. |
| Specific personal authorship, novelty, adoption or clinical impact | Source functionality alone does not establish these. Use verified contribution history and externally supportable evidence before claiming them. |
